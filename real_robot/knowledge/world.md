CodeWorld uses meters in the task card world frame. world.snapshot() gives a consistent geometry revision. Use snapshot.transform(id), world.bounds(id), world.mesh(id), world.parts(id), world.nearest_surface(id, points_W) and world.target(...) to compute geometry. world.live.position(id) retains dependencies; world.compute(lambda s: ...) recomputes from a current snapshot. Resolve expressions for a pose immediately before the physical call. world.fork(name) and branch.set_hypothesis(...) support hypothetical poses. world.publish_parts and world.update_parts change part geometry or transforms. world.preview_plan forecasts access for later stages; inspect actual controller path feedback separately.

Follow read–action–update. After motion inspect paired real RGB and same-camera geometry. Call world.commit_step(ctx['interaction']['pending_update']['capture'], expected_revision=ctx['revision'], note=..., poses=..., held=..., relations=...) after reading the latest context. Supply T_W_object, translation_observed_axes and orientation_observed_axes for observed poses. Use unresolved for occlusion. A prediction from hand kinematics is provisional. Check grasp_review and pose_refresh; visually correct the payload transform when observations disagree. For an image-time held pose use time_basis='image'. After release observe the object independently of the fingers and verify again after withdrawal. Use world.compare_views(capture) to inspect corrections.

For calibrated multiview RGB use poses={id: {'image_points': {'mode': 'rigid', 'points': [{'local_xyz': [x,y,z], 'pixels': {view_a: [u,v], view_b: [u,v]}}, ...]}}}. Choose at least three non-collinear mesh-local landmarks, each matched in two current views at original resolution. Translation-only mode can use one matched landmark. Inspect fit_failed and sensor_validation residuals. Outside pending steps use world.integrate_rgb(capture, poses=..., expected_revision=..., note=...). Single-view and occluded geometry may leave depth unresolved.

Use a fresh context for each action. Example review:
    ctx = world.read_context()
    capture = ctx['interaction']['pending_update']['capture']
    world.commit_step(capture, expected_revision=ctx['revision'], note=description,
                      poses=observed_poses, held={arm: observed_payload}, unresolved=uncertain)
Use held={arm: None} after observed release or loss. A carried transform extrapolated from the hand can guide a search. A new visible pose corrects the grasp offset; a deferred or failed fit leaves uncertainty. Check rejected_held, recovery_history and the updated revision. Use disturbed for displaced neighbors with unknown poses. A relation such as supported or inserted records an observation; check the object's stability after withdrawal. Leave stable background objects unchanged.

For reusable geometry, s = world.snapshot(); s.local_point(id, local_xyz) and s.transform(id) use a single revision. world.target(lambda s: ...) computes xyz, and world.compute(lambda s: ...) can retain other numeric dependencies. Converting a live value to an array or list freezes that estimate. Use world.publish_parts(id, {part_id: {'source_faces': indices, 'T_object_part': T}}, expected_revision=...) for decompositions of the original mesh, or world.update_parts(id, {part_id: T}, ...) for changed part transforms. Base dimension or topology changes on visible geometry. For access checks preview candidate stages with world.preview_plan([{'name': stage, 'poses': {id: T}, 'access': [future_id]}]). Inspect calibration, occlusion and hidden surfaces when interpreting small clearances.
